# SubTrack Feature-Opportunity Research Evidence

## Research Boundary

This is qualitative, public-community research. The discussions below are **firsthand anecdotes**, not representative market measurements. They are used to identify recurring decision and memory problems that a privacy-first tracker can address without bank aggregation, inbox access, provider credentials, or provider-side control.

| Source | Date | Direct user-reported signal | Product implication |
|---|---:|---|---|
| [Reddit: subscription fatigue](https://www.reddit.com/r/SaaS/comments/1qz7jj3/im_sick_of_renting_my_life_subscription_fatigue/) | February 2026 | Participants describe a growing mental load, forgotten or rarely used services, difficulty recalling what each subscription does, and distrust of management apps that require bank access. | Support manual, private decision support: value check-ins, use self-report, renewal review prompts, and a no-bank-access explanation. |
| [Figma Forum: monthly cancellation](https://forum.figma.com/ask-the-community-7/how-do-i-cancel-my-monthly-subscription-48771) | December 2025 | A user asks how to cancel a subscription; the thread points participants to provider renewal/cancellation guidance, showing that billing ownership and the correct management destination are not always obvious. | Preserve explicit billing-source routing, official management handoffs, and a local cancellation checklist/evidence timeline. |
| [1Password Community: price increase](https://www.1password.community/1password-at-home-31/what-justifies-the-huge-subscription-price-increase-24103) | February 2026 | Users describe material annual price increases, short notice, uncertainty about whether the added value justifies the change, and the need to evaluate alternatives or migrate before renewal. | Support expected-charge review, cost-change reasons, decision-window reminders, a value check-in, and a user-maintained alternative shortlist; do not claim live price monitoring. |
| [Spotify Community: family-plan payment issue](https://community.spotify.com/t5/Premium-Family/Premium-Family-Payment-Issue-A-New-Family-Was-Created-Instead-of/td-p/7154363) | September 2025 | A family-plan owner reports that payment trouble led to a new plan rather than a renewal, then needed to understand membership transfer, inactive-plan recovery, and member eligibility implications. | Expand local household records with plan owner, member invitations/eligibility notes, payment-owner contact reminder, and a transition checklist; do not attempt to modify provider family membership. |
| [Reddit: missed free-trial cancellation](https://www.reddit.com/r/FlutterDev/comments/1vex9d1/how_do_you_handle_i_forgot_to_cancel_my_free/) | August 2026 | A developer describes a customer who believed a short free trial had been cancelled but was charged an annual renewal; replies discuss how short trial windows and delayed recognition raise refund and trust friction. | Add a trial-to-paid decision plan: anticipated paid amount, advance review reminders, a user-confirmed “keep or exit” decision, and an official cancellation handoff. Do not claim it stops billing or secures refunds. |
| [Google Play Community: unknown charge](https://support.google.com/googleplay/thread/449379268/unknown-google-charge-unable-to-find-the-purchase-or-subscription-requesting-help-to-identify-the?hl=en) | July 2026 | A user cannot match a charge to a purchase or subscription. Community guidance points them through multiple activity, subscription, order-history, and unauthorized-transaction paths. | Provide a local charge-recognition worksheet and billing-source triage checklist with official platform paths. Never diagnose fraud, access payment histories, or file disputes for the user. |
| [Adobe Community: renewal reminder](https://community.adobe.com/questions-6/no-reminder-on-renewal-1556394) | April 2026 | A user says they were unaware that renewal was due and could not locate the expected provider notice; the thread notes that notices can be affected by inbox handling. | Provide device-local reminder delivery status, multi-stage pre-renewal review prompts, a reminder test action, and a manual decision record rather than depending on provider email. |
| [Figma Forum: annual seat downgrade](https://forum.figma.com/ask-the-community-7/annual-billing-change-and-downgrade-seats-52717) | April 2026 | A user wants to reduce annual seats before renewal and needs to understand when access and billing change. The response distinguishes immediate access changes from the next billing term. | Add plan-change timing notes, a renewal decision window, action checklist, and a local estimate of the next-term impact. Do not promise provider-side changes or live seat state. |

## Initial Recurring Problems

The evidence now supports ten themes: **subscription fatigue**, **uncertain continued value**, **cancellation-path ambiguity**, **price-change decision pressure**, **household-plan dependency**, **trial deadline risk**, **charge-recognition friction**, **reminder reliability**, **plan-change timing**, and **privacy distrust**.
