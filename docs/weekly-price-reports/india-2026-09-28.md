## Policy status meanings

`reviewable-india-source` means a provider-owned page explicitly scoped to India/INR states an ordinary recurring price. `promotion-or-eligibility-limited` records trials, introductory prices, student, household, seat, or time-limited terms separately. `context-required` records provider-owned pages that show another currency or require India/account context. `manual-only` records unreadable, gated, placeholder, or unavailable pricing. `no-change-confirmed` is reserved for an already approved India reference that is re-confirmed.
