## Policy and status vocabulary

This report follows `docs/weekly-india-price-report-policy.md`. Results use `reviewable-india-source`, `promotion-or-eligibility-limited`, `context-required`, `manual-only`, and `no-change-confirmed`. A proposed catalog change always requires human review; an observed provider price is not a user’s actual billed amount.

## Executive summary

All **23 current catalog services** were assessed through directly opened provider-owned public pages. Three services—**Amazon Prime, Spotify, and Apple Music**—exposed India/INR recurring wording on their public pages. Several other providers exposed USD, placeholders, bundle prices, tax-excluded prices, seat-based prices, trial offers, or account-gated pricing; those observations are not treated as India prices. No currency conversion or inferred INR value was used.

The safest outcome is a **manual-review queue**, not an automatic catalog update. The strongest India/INR candidates are Amazon Prime’s ₹299/month and ₹1,499/year Prime tiers, Spotify’s ₹299/month Platinum tier and ₹139/month Standard renewal wording after its introductory offer, and Apple Music’s ₹139/month Individual and ₹229/month Family tiers. Student, trial, household, bundle, tax, seat, and promotional conditions remain separate. The report does not modify `lib/catalog.ts` or any user record.

## Manual-review summary

### Proposed changes requiring review

1. **Amazon Prime:** Review mapping the current `monthly` reference (`US$14.99/month`) to the India page’s “₹299 /month,” and the current `annual` reference (`US$139/year`) to “₹1,499 /year.” Prime Lite (“₹799 /year”) is a new India tier; Prime Shopping Edition (“₹399 /year,” with nearby “₹499 ₹399” offer wording) must remain separate until its recurring-versus-promotional status is confirmed. Prime Video is shown as a bundled benefit, not a standalone price.
2. **Spotify:** Review mapping the current `premium` reference (`US$12.99/month`) to the India page’s “₹139/month after” wording, while keeping “₹139 for 2 months” as an introductory offer. The India Platinum tier (“₹299 / month,” up to three people at the same address) does not automatically map to the existing `family` reference and requires plan-identity review. Student wording remains eligibility-limited.
3. **Apple Music:** Review mapping the current `individual` reference (`US$11.99/month`) to “₹139/month” and `family` (`US$19.99/month`) to “₹229/month.” The page separately states “₹69/month” for verified students and one-month trials for new subscribers; these should not overwrite ordinary plan references.

### No automatic change proposed

ChatGPT, Claude, Netflix, YouTube Premium, Microsoft 365, Google One, Notion, Headspace, Coursera, PlayStation Plus, Perplexity, Grammarly, Dropbox, Canva, Disney+, Hulu, Audible, Strava, Xbox Game Pass, and Duolingo did not expose a clear India/INR recurring price that can be safely mapped to the existing reference labels in this run. Their rows preserve exact non-INR, placeholder, gated, tax, seat, bundle, trial, or missing-price evidence and remain `context-required`, `manual-only`, or `promotion-or-eligibility-limited`.

**Manual application rule:** A reviewer must confirm service, plan identity, India scope, currency, recurring cadence, tax treatment, and effective date before changing a catalog reference.
