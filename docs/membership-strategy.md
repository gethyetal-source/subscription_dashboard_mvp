# Membership strategy (internal)

These ideas are product-planning notes, not available memberships or customer offers. Keep them outside the application.

- **Free core + Plus:** Keep manual tracking, renewal dates, and local storage useful for free. Test demand for deeper history, flexible reminders, and exports. Illustrative discussion prices: $3.99/month or $34.99/year.
- **Household membership:** Consider an optional household tier only after validating demand and privacy expectations. Illustrative discussion prices: $6.99/month or $59.99/year per household. Encrypted backup is a possible future benefit, not a current feature.
- **Transparent referrals:** Disclose any qualifying referral fees. Do not put sponsored placements in cancellation or renewal-critical flows.
- **Benefits licenses:** Consider employer or community access without exposing individual subscription records or spending.

Validate value before building paid tiers. No live checkout, membership, referral program, or business license is implemented.

The developer theme preview lives in `components/dev/theme-lab.tsx`, outside the public route directory.
